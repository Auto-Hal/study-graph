The first Western Art History Objective uses the generic Objective v2 archive, issuance, routing, Receipt, and SRS boundaries already proven by Kuzushiji and Philosophy. It adds one Git-owned text-only exercise: `western-art-history.cromlech.term-recall`.

`STUDY_GRAPH_WESTERN_ART_HISTORY_PILOT_ISSUANCE_ENABLED` defaults OFF. Only exact lowercase `true`, together with global Objective v2 issuance, permits new Cromlech Objective issuance. When selected, Cromlech is removed from legacy scheduling before candidate selection. A not-due result or issuer failure produces no Cromlech card and no legacy fallback.

The issued card is always reconstructed from the persisted immutable archive, including active-opportunity reuse. First acceptance grades that persisted revision, fresh-reads the Western Art History Notion graph, applies the existing direct-related-lecture/date Scope policy to the pinned Cromlech term, and leaves the SRS decision to the common Objective v2 database writer. Accepted retry returns the stored Receipt before project routing, grading, Scope, or epoch evaluation.

Turning the Art issuance flag OFF stops new Art Objective issuance. Already-issued Art v2 instances still use the deployed context-aware acceptance and Receipt-v2 path. This slice does not add offline prefetch, change the durable Objective outbox, write legacy Review state, or change the database schema.
